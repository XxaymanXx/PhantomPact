Phantom Pact play logs land here: one text file each time you play (playlog-<date>-<time>.txt), the last 12 kept.
If something goes wrong, send the one of that session with your report, by the date and time in its name (after a
restart the newest is the new session's: take the one before it). They hold no address, name or password.
PlayLog = Off in PhantomPact.ini turns them off. You can delete these files at any time.
